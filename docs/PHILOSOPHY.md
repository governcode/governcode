# What GovernCode stands for

1. **You hold the controls.** AI does the work; you make the calls. Every step that matters, like running a command, changing files outside the plan, or handing work to another AI, stops and waits for your yes. Nothing lands in your project until you've seen it and accepted it, and you can always undo it.

2. **You ship it, you own it.** GovernCode exists to make building software with AI more approachable: fewer surprises, clearer reviews, an easy undo. It does not make anyone less responsible. Whatever you accept is yours: read the diff, understand the code, run the tests, and know what you are shipping before you ship it. The AI can write it; only you can answer for it.

3. **Deny by default, fail closed.** The sandbox is enforced by the operating system, not a suggestion the AI is asked to follow. If a protection can't be enforced on your machine, GovernCode refuses to start and tells you exactly why, rather than running without it.

4. **Any model can lead.** You choose which AI leads and which ones help: Claude, Codex, Gemini, Grok, or a model on your own machine. No lock-in, and no favourite vendor built into the design.

5. **Honest about limits.** We tell you what we measure, how, and where the edges are. A usage limit is a measured hold, not a promise, and we say so. When something can't be guaranteed, the docs say that plainly instead of hiding it.

6. **Free and open, forever.** MIT licensed. No paid tier, no telemetry, no account needed. Your code, your keys and your history stay on your machine.

7. **Built with AI, openly.** AI writes much of GovernCode, and we say exactly which AI did what. A human reviews everything that ships.

8. **Small, boring, verifiable.** Few moving parts, plain code, and a test for every rule that matters. If a clever trick and a boring solution both work, we pick the boring one.

9. **Respect, always.** This project is about the work, not about opinions of people's choices. Love AI or distrust it, prefer one OS or another, use this project or a different one: all welcome, and none of it needs defending here. We discuss code, designs and ideas, never the people behind them or the tools they like. Critique the project as hard as you want; treat everyone with respect.
